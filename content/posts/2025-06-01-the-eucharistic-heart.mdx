The Eucharist is the source and summit of the Christian life. This is not merely a theological statement — it is an invitation.

## The Real Presence

When we approach the altar, we are not receiving a symbol. We are receiving the Body, Blood, Soul, and Divinity of Jesus Christ. This truth has the power to transform everything about how we live.

> "I am the living bread that came down from heaven; whoever eats this bread will live forever." — John 6:51

## A Daily Encounter

The gift of daily Mass, if accessible to you, is one of the greatest graces available to a Catholic. Even when the day is ordinary, the Mass is extraordinary. Christ offers Himself anew — and we are invited to offer ourselves with Him.

## An Act of Thanksgiving

The word *Eucharist* comes from the Greek *eucharistia*, meaning thanksgiving. Every time we receive, we are drawn into a posture of gratitude — not only for the gift of the Eucharist itself, but for all of God's goodness in our lives.

May we never receive unworthily, and may we never receive without wonder.
